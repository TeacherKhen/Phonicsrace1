HOW TO PLAY

  • • Two teams race along the phonics path!
  • • Click MOVE T1 or MOVE T2 to jump step-by-step.
  • • Read each word correctly to move forward.
  • • The fastest team to reach the last word wins!

TEACHER SETUP

  • • Drag word boxes on screen directly with touch or mouse to reposition them anywhere!
  • • Click EDIT ⚙️ to customize word text or add/delete targets.
  • • Adjust music volume & text font size in the settings panel.
